Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 22, 2026

We at AnimeCatch ("we", "the App", "Development Team") are fully committed to protecting your privacy and personal data. This privacy policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our application and services.

1. Information We Collect

To provide a seamless user experience, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Registration Information (Personal Data): When creating an account, we ask for basic information such as (Name, Email, Password, and Date of Birth).
  • Profile Data: Additional information you voluntarily choose to add, such as (Nickname, Bio, Geolocation, and Favorite Anime).
  • User-Generated Content: Comments, replies, ratings, and likes you share within the app.
  • Usage and Device Data: Technical information may be collected automatically, such as (Device type, Operating System, and unique device identifiers) to improve app performance and resolve technical issues.

2. How Do We Use Your Information?

We do not sell or rent your data to any third party. We use the collected information exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing your personal account in the app.
  • Providing core features of the app (such as displaying profiles and comments).
  • Communicating with you to send verification emails or security alerts.
  • Improving and developing the app's performance based on how users interact with it.

3. Sharing Data with Third-Party Services

To ensure service stability, we rely on trusted cloud service providers with global security standards (e.g., Appwrite Servers / Databases). These entities process data on our behalf under strict confidentiality terms and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

4. Data Security

We implement advanced security and encryption measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, modification, or disclosure. Passwords are completely hashed so that no one, including the development team, can view them.

5. User Rights (Controlling Your Data)

You have full control over your data:

  • Access and Modification: You can edit your profile data at any time through the app settings.
  • Data Deletion: You have the right to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated data from our servers via the deletion option within the app.

6. Children's Privacy

Our application is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the legal age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we discover that we have collected a child's data without parental consent, we will delete this information immediately.

7. Changes to the Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to keep pace with technical or legal changes. We will notify you of any material changes via the app or email. Your continued use of the app after an update constitutes your agreement to the revised policy.
